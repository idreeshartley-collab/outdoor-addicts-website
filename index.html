Choose your Cape Town classic

Two iconic routes, guided with local knowledge, safety awareness and a smooth booking experience.

Lion’s Head

Lion’s Head Sunrise, Sunset or Summit

A Cape Town favourite with big views, a rewarding summit and a shorter hiking experience than Table Mountain.

  • • Difficulty: Moderate
  • • Distance: ±5km round trip
  • • Duration: ±3–4 hours
  • • Best for sunrise, sunset and classic summit views

Table Mountain

Table Mountain Platteklip Gorge or India Venster

Choose the route that matches your confidence, fitness and appetite for adventure, from a direct summit route to a more technical mountain experience.

  • • Platteklip Gorge: direct, steep and non-technical
  • • India Venster: adventurous, exposed and technical
  • • Duration: ±2–4 hours depending on route
  • • Route suitability confirmed before booking
