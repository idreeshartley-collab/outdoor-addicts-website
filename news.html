Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about hiking in Cape Town

Quick answers to some of the questions we hear most often from guests planning a Table Mountain or Lion’s Head hike.

How difficult is hiking Table Mountain?

Table Mountain is a proper mountain hike with sustained climbing. Even the more direct routes can feel like a long stair workout, and the difficulty depends on your fitness, route and the day’s conditions. Read our local guide to hiking Table Mountain before deciding what suits you.

Which is easier: Lion’s Head or Table Mountain?

For most visitors, Lion’s Head is the shorter and less demanding outing, although it still includes steep sections and some scrambling. Table Mountain generally involves more elevation gain and a longer sustained climb. Your fitness, comfort with heights and chosen route all matter.

Can beginners hike Table Mountain?

Yes, many reasonably active beginners can hike Table Mountain, but choosing the right route is important. We look at your recent hiking experience, general fitness and comfort on steep terrain before recommending an option.

Do I need a guide to hike Table Mountain?

A guide is not compulsory on every route, but local knowledge can make route choice, navigation, weather decisions and difficult sections much easier to manage. It also means you can focus on the experience while learning about the mountain, its plants, geology, history and viewpoints.

What should I wear and bring for a Cape Town hike?

Wear comfortable hiking or trail shoes with good grip. Bring water, sun protection and a warm or wind-resistant layer even when the day starts clear, because mountain conditions can change quickly. Longer hikes may also require snacks or a light lunch.

What happens if the weather is bad on the day of my hike?

We assess the route and current conditions rather than relying on a single forecast. Strong wind, heavy rain, lightning or unsafe mountain conditions may mean changing the start time, choosing a more suitable route or postponing the hike.

Can I hike up Table Mountain and take the cable car down?

Often, yes. However, the cableway operates weather permitting, so it should never be treated as a guaranteed descent. We plan the route, timing and fitness requirements with the possibility that hiking down may still be necessary.

Should I choose Platteklip Gorge or India Venster?

Platteklip Gorge is more direct and straightforward to navigate, while India Venster is more adventurous and includes scrambling and greater exposure. Our India Venster vs Platteklip Gorge guide explains the differences in detail.
