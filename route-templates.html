Outdoor Addicts route planning notes

Internal reference only. Not linked in public navigation.

Kasteelspoort → Upper Cableway

Start: Theresa Avenue / Camps Bay
Finish: Upper Cableway
Duration: ±5–6 hrs
Terrain: steep ascent, mild scrambling, long upper traverse
Suitability: strong fitness / endurance
Return: cableway when operating; hiking descent must remain a contingency
Scheduled: suitable; sunrise-aligned start
Fees: cableway excluded.

Skeleton Gorge → Upper Cableway

Start: Kirstenbosch
Finish: Upper Cableway
Duration: ±5–6 hrs
Elevation: roughly 900 m for the extended guided route; verify exact track before publishing as a fixed figure
Terrain: forest, stream, ladders, boulder ravine, reservoir, plateau
Rain: wet rock / high water can make gorge unsafe; guide decision after rainfall
Variant: reservoir → Nursery Ravine descent back to Kirstenbosch
Scheduled: suitable; 08:00 start
Fees: Kirstenbosch entrance and cableway excluded.

India Venster

Start: Lower Cableway area
Finish: Upper Cableway
Terrain: steep trail, scrambling, exposure
Suitability: strong fitness and comfort with heights
Weather: avoid unsafe wind / wet exposed rock.

Platteklip Gorge

Start: Tafelberg Road
Finish: Upper Cableway
Terrain: sustained stone-step ascent
Suitability: active hikers; lower technical demand than India Venster
Weather: heat and exposure to sun are important considerations.
