The experience

A hidden world above Camps Bay.

Climb Kasteelspoort to the famous Diving Board before traversing the Twelve Apostles to one of Table Mountain’s best-hidden gems.

Inside Tranquility Cracks, huge sandstone fissures shelter moss-covered rocks and indigenous yellowwood trees, creating a calm world that feels completely removed from the open mountain outside.

We return through Corridor Ravine and the historic Pipe Track, passing remnants of Cape Town’s old mountain water pipeline and a cave-like overhang before completing the loop.

The journey

What the experience feels like

01

Climb Kasteelspoort

A sustained ascent from Theresa Avenue opens huge Atlantic and Camps Bay views.

02

The Diving Board

A short exploration and photo stop at one of the Twelve Apostles’ best-known rock formations.

03

Across the Apostles

Enjoy a flatter upper-mountain traverse with rocky terrain and short scrambles.

04

Enter Tranquility Cracks

Find the inconspicuous entrance and explore mossy corridors, yellowwoods and giant boulders.

05

Descend Corridor Ravine

Leave the hidden cracks and descend back toward the Atlantic-facing slopes.

06

Historic Pipe Track

Follow the old water pipeline route past our final cave-like overhang and back to the start.

Weather & safety

A good level of hiking fitness is required. Expect a sustained climb, uneven mountain terrain and short scrambling sections. The route is weather dependent and may be adjusted for safety.
