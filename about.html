About us

Built from a love for Cape Town’s trails

Outdoor Addicts is a Cape Town-based outdoor adventure company established in 2022, created to help guests experience our mountains safely, personally and memorably.

Meet Idrees

My name is Idrees. I grew up in the Cape Flats of Cape Town and developed a love for nature from a young age while exploring trails with friends and family.

That passion grew into a career in adventure guiding, where I get to share Cape Town’s landscapes, stories and outdoor experiences with people from around the world.

What we stand for

Outdoor Addicts focuses on safe, well-managed and personal hiking experiences. We are safety-conscious, respect mountain etiquette, and encourage guests to connect with nature responsibly.

More than just a hike

Every hike is an opportunity to share local insights, help guests feel comfortable on the mountain, and create memories that last beyond the summit photo.
